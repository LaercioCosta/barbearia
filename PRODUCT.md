# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
delegated: semantic HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript for a single responsive landing page without adding a build system.

## Users
Prospective local barbershop customers comparing services and looking to book or find the shop.

## Product Purpose
Present a premium barbershop and help visitors understand services, team, location and booking steps.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Single-page anchor navigation, responsive menu, WhatsApp booking, gallery, FAQ and location links. Phone, address, hours, social accounts, team credentials and real reviews are undecided and must remain configurable rather than fabricated.

## Brand Commitments
The supplied mockup is binding: dark editorial barbershop, warm cinematic photography, restrained aged gold, condensed display titles and the displayed section order and composition.

## Evidence on Hand
One supplied visual mockup. No verified commercial details, original photo assets, team profiles or customer reviews supplied.

## Product Principles
- Make services and booking easy to find.
- Preserve the approved visual reference across desktop and mobile.
- Do not present illustrative business details or testimonials as facts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard-operable navigation and controls, visible focus, descriptive image text, and reduced-motion support.
